# ThinkBuddy character direction

Original soft clay chibi characters, large expressive heads, rounded bodies and tiny feet. Shared proportions, matte material, soft lighting, warm approachable expressions. Neutral age presentation; personality comes from motivations rather than gender, ethnicity, or costume.

Buddy is the permanent facilitator: a mint-teal creature with leaf-shaped ears, cream face and belly, coral cheeks, dark oval eyes, and tiny rounded limbs. Buddy wants the user to reach an independent conclusion, fears an overlooked assumption, asks concise questions, and listens before summarizing. Its silhouette, ears, face, and mint color never change.

The reusable atlas contains Buddy plus a sage-clothed leaf holder, a yellow-cardigan notebook holder, a lilac-clothed star holder, a blue-overall plant holder, and a peach fox with a scarf. These appearances are not fixed professional roles. Model-generated identities select an appearance independently of their reasoning responsibility.

Palette: teal #468B79, sage #669A89, peach #C99681, lilac #9B91BA, sand #D6AD62, blue #7E9BB8.

Expression vocabulary: welcoming, curious, thinking, surprised, concerned, amused, doubtful, determined. Pose vocabulary: listening, speaking, questioning, reflecting, welcoming. The initial atlas has one friendly pose per character; motion conveys idle, selected, and speaking states. It does not claim to contain full pose/turnaround sheets.
